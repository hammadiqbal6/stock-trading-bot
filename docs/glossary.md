# Glossary

- **PDT** — Pattern Day Trader rule (US): &lt;$25k equity limited to 3 day trades in 5 business days.
- **DSR** — Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey & López de Prado).
- **PBO** — Probability of Backtest Overfitting; we expose a simplified proxy.
- **Holdout** — Untouched final period after LLM cutoff for honest OOS check.
- **Advisory layer** — Multi-agent LLM research notes that cannot trade.
- **paper_sim** — Local in-memory broker when Alpaca keys are absent.
- **IEX** — Alpaca Basic realtime equity feed.
